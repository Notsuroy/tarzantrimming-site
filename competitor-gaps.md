## Where the field pressures us (gaps worth closing)

### Gap 1: Tree permit and bylaw CONTENT (highest autonomous lever, no capability claim needed)
Every corridor city protects private trees and requires a permit (plus an arborist report) to remove a protected one. Toronto's Private Tree By-law (Municipal Code Chapter 813) protects any private tree 30 cm in diameter or larger measured at 1.4 m, requires an arborist report detailing size, species, condition and reason, runs a 30-business-day review, and carries fines from $500 up to $100,000 per tree (source: City of Toronto tree-permit pages, fetched 2026-06-18). Mississauga, Oakville, Burlington and Hamilton each have their own private-tree bylaws with their own thresholds (confirm exact numbers per city before publishing specifics).

This is a HIGH-intent informational lever: a homeowner Googling "do I need a permit to remove a tree in Toronto" is a removal/pruning lead. Doc Holliday and Community Tree Care surface this; most of the thin field does not. **A permit/bylaw explainer (per-city) is winnable content that funnels straight into the pruning and limb-removal money pages, and it needs ZERO service-line or credential claim, so the cron can build it autonomously.**

**Action (autonomous, cron-doable):** a "Tree removal permits in [city]" guide or blog post, Toronto first (best-sourced), then Mississauga/Oakville. Informational, links to `/services/tree-pruning/` + `/services/limb-removal/` + `/contact/`. Pair with the existing blog pipeline.

### Gap 2: Arborist reports / tree-permit applications (highest revenue, MOST gated, Nathan decision)
Because every corridor city requires an arborist report to remove a protected tree, "arborist report [city]" is recurring, high-value, high-intent demand. Doc Holliday and Community Tree Care both offer it. An arborist report must be authored by a qualified professional (ISA Certified Arborist or Registered Professional Forester). Nathan's Davey Tree background may well qualify him.

**Action (NEEDS NATHAN CONFIRMATION, do NOT build until confirmed):** does Nathan hold (or is he pursuing) ISA Certified Arborist status, and does he want to offer paid arborist reports / permit-application support? If YES, this is the single highest-value differentiated service line in this market and warrants a dedicated service page. If NO, we still publish the Gap 1 informational content and route the actual report work elsewhere. This is a genuine owner decision, not a cron build.

### Gap 3: City / service-area pages (structural gap, P2, coverage must be honest)
Tarzan lists seven service cities in `site.json` but has NO dedicated area pages (`areas.json` does not exist; only a single `service-areas.astro`). Every corridor competitor targets multiple cities. Dedicated `/areas/toronto/`, `/areas/mississauga/`, etc. pages would capture city-level intent that the single combined page cannot.

**Action (P2, autonomous IF coverage is genuine):** build `areas.json` + city pages for the seven service cities, with locally accurate copy (real neighbourhoods, the relevant city's tree bylaw, common species). **Honesty check:** only publish a city page for a city Nathan can actually service by appointment. Confirm the seven-city list is real before building seven pages (a brand-new evenings-and-weekends operator may realistically cover a tighter radius first). If the real radius is narrower, build pages for the genuine coverage and trim `serviceCities`.

### Gap 4: Stump grinding / removal (service-line gap, Nathan decision)
Every competitor in the set offers stump grinding. Tarzan does not. It is the most common "and can you also..." follow-on to a removal, and a frequent standalone job.

**Action (NEEDS NATHAN DECISION):** does Tarzan have access to a stump grinder (owned or rented) and want stump work? If YES, add a `/services/stump-grinding/` page. If NO, decide whether to say so or stay silent. Do not assert the capability either way until confirmed.

### Gap 5: Emergency / storm-damage limb response (honest framing only)
Doc Holliday and Community Tree Care advertise storm / emergency response (some with 24-hour framing). Storm-downed and hanging limbs are urgent, high-intent work. Tarzan is evenings-and-weekends by appointment.

**Action (honest version, Nathan decision on availability):** a "storm-damaged and hazardous limb removal" angle is legitimate (the existing blog pipeline already has a storm-limb-cleanup topic). Frame it honestly: prompt assessment and removal by appointment, fastest available slot, NO "24/7" or "always available" claim (banned, and arborist work is genuinely not a middle-of-the-night service). Confirm with Nathan how fast he can realistically respond to an urgent limb before promising any window.

### Gap 6: Stated trust credentials (the clearest OPENING, Nathan confirmation)
The field is thin on stated credentials. An owner-led operator who can honestly state "ISA Certified Arborist, fully insured (liability + WSIB), Davey-trained, [N] years of tree work" would out-signal most of the corridor on trust, which is exactly what converts a quote request.

**Action (NEEDS NATHAN CONFIRMATION before ANYTHING goes on the site):** confirm the exact, true, currently-holdable claims, ISA certification status, liability insurance, WSIB coverage, and how to honestly describe the Davey-Tree experience (employment vs endorsement, do not imply Davey endorses Tarzan). Then add a trust block sitewide. Until confirmed, claim nothing. This mirrors the BRF "no certification claim until obtained" guardrail.

### Gap 7: Hedge trimming (low-barrier add-on, Nathan decision)
Common, low-equipment add-on that most competitors bundle. **Action (Nathan decision):** does Tarzan want hedge work? If yes, it is a cheap service-page add. Low priority.

### Gap 8: Google Business Profile + local pack (REAL owner lever, NOT tenant-bound)
Unlike the rank-and-rent shells (where GBP comes with a future tenant), Tarzan is a real business and Nathan can and should claim and verify a Google Business Profile. The local pack converts far better than organic at the bottom of the SERP, and for a brand-new domain with no organic authority, GBP is likely the FASTEST path to actual calls.

**Action (Nathan / Suroy action, high priority):** claim and verify a GBP for Tarzan Trimming (service-area business, hidden address is fine and correct for a mobile operator per Google's SAB guidance). This is the highest-converting near-term lever for a new site and is genuinely actionable now.
